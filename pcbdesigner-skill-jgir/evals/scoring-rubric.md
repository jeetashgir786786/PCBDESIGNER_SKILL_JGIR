# Skill evaluation rubric

Evaluate outputs against known answer keys or expert review. Do not score fluency as engineering quality.

Suggested weighted score:
- Electrical correctness: 25%
- Calculation quality: 15%
- Component/datasheet grounding: 15%
- Layout reasoning: 15%
- Manufacturability: 10%
- Verification evidence: 10%
- Safety/risk: 5%
- Communication/instruction adherence: 5%

Record baseline (without skill) and skilled performance for each model. Track regressions and false claims. Require human review for safety-critical or fabrication-release decisions.
